What it does

It builds the session. You just train.

Not a logbook you have to fill in. Not a video library to scroll. You say what you want, it decides what covers the most ground in the time you have, and it shows you how.

The ask box

Plain words in

Location, muscle groups, movement count, effort, conditioning, and lifts you name by name. A duration counts too — “45 minutes” becomes six movements.

Generator

Coverage first

It fills the sheet by maximising new muscle coverage while keeping the movement patterns balanced, so nothing gets trained three times and nothing gets skipped.

3D rig

See the movement

Every lift comes with an articulated 3D figure running the actual motion, plus a heat map of exactly which muscles it loads. No guessing from a name.

Anywhere

Home, gym, or nothing

Define your home kit once and it will never prescribe a lift you can't load. Body Weight mode strips it to you and the floor — a hotel room, a park, ten minutes.

Build the week

A whole split at once

Pick a split and it generates every session in it. Save the split; the movements re-roll each week, so the same routine keeps landing on different work.

Points & standings

Something to chase

Sessions bank points, streaks multiply them, and an optional account puts you on local, national and global boards. Your training never leaves the device.

How it goes

Three taps to a session

Say what you want

Type it, or tap a preset. The workload panel fills in underneath so you can see exactly what it heard before anything is built.

Forge it

One press builds the sheet — movements, sets, reps, rest, RPE and tempo, ordered so the heavy work happens while you are fresh.

Train and bank it

Tick sets as you go. Finishing banks the points, extends the streak, and feeds the next session's targeting so under-trained muscles come back around.

On your phone

It installs like an app

There is nothing to download. Open it in Safari and add it to your home screen — it gets its own icon, opens full screen with no browser chrome, and works with no signal.

iPhone & iPad
  1. Open Fatcamp in Safari — it has to be Safari, not Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow out of the top).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch it from the icon. Full screen, offline, no address bar.

On a Mac or PC it just runs in the browser — or use Install in Chrome's address bar for a windowed app.
