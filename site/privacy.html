Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

The short version. Everything you log stays on your device. You can use the whole app without an account. If you make one, the only things that leave your device are a handle, a rough region, and your point totals — so a leaderboard can exist. The only measurement is a set of anonymous usage counts ("a session was forged today") connected to nobody. There is no advertising, no third-party tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

Who this is

Fatcamp is built and run by Tater Tot Studios. Contact: tanman0495@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

By default the app has no server. Your profile, your logged sessions, your streak, your personal bests, your equipment list, your movement limitations and your settings are stored in your browser's local storage, or inside the app's own container on iOS. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Clearing site data, deleting the app, or using Reset all progress erases it permanently — there is no copy for us to restore.

What leaves your device, and only if you sign in

An account is optional and exists for one reason: leaderboards. Creating one sends an email address and a password to our authentication provider. After that, the app syncs:

Your individual sessions are enforced private at the database level, not merely hidden in the interface. What you lifted, how much, and which movements you chose are never published. Nobody sees another person's training.

Anonymous usage counts

The app and this site record a small number of anonymous events — "someone opened the app", "a session was forged", "a session was banked", "this poster code brought someone here". Each event is a name, a timestamp, and a random install identifier generated on your device and connected to nothing else. No email, no name, no device fingerprint, no location, and no link to your training data — if you also have a leaderboard account, the two are never joined. These counts exist so we can answer questions like "do the posters work" and "do people come back", in aggregate. They are never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no third-party analytics tool is involved — events go only to our own database.

What is never collected

  • No third-party analytics. The only telemetry is the anonymous usage counts described above, and they identify nobody.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no third-party trackers.
  • No location. "Region" is a value you pick from a list; the app does not read GPS.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera. If you upload a photo of your gym, it is read in your browser to detect equipment and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with anyone for any purpose.

Apple Health (iOS app, when available)

The iOS version can ask permission to read workouts recorded by other apps, so sessions you did elsewhere still count toward your streak, and to write your Fatcamp sessions back to Health. This is opt-in through Apple's own permission dialog and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. Health data is read on the device and never transmitted to us or anyone else — it is not synced, not stored on a server, and not used for anything but your streak and your log.

Service providers

Accounts and leaderboards run on Supabase, which stores the account data and anonymous usage counts described above on our behalf. The website and app are served by GitHub Pages, which processes standard web-server request logs. If the app is distributed through the App Store, Apple provides download and crash statistics in aggregate. There are no other providers.

Children

Fatcamp is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Resistance training programming for a growing body is a conversation for a parent and a doctor, not an app.

Your choices

  • Use it signed out. Every feature except leaderboards works with no account.
  • Delete your local data. Reset all progress, in the footer of the app.
  • Delete your account. Email the address above and it will be removed, along with your leaderboard row and session records, within 30 days.
  • Export. Profiles can be exported to a file from the profile panel.

Security, honestly stated

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Account data is protected by row-level rules that make one person's sessions unreadable to another. That said, this is a small independent app, not a bank — do not put anything in a workout title you would mind being associated with your account.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the notice appears in the app. Continuing to use Fatcamp after that means the new version applies.

Not medical advice

Fatcamp generates training suggestions from rules, not from any knowledge of your health. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Warm up, use judgement, and talk to a doctor before starting a training programme.
